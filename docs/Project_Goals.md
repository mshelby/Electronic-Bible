# Project Goals

A premium, offline electronic Bible reader built with quiet-quality principles — prioritizing honest engineering, acoustic performance, and timeless design.

## Key Objectives

- **Offline-first experience** with full text library access
- **Privacy-preserving** — no cloud dependency, all data stays local
- **Clean, minimalist interface** inspired by classic design
- **Scalable architecture** for future expansion (multiple languages, fonts, themes)
- **Text-to-speech** with natural voice synthesis
- **Cross-device sync** via encrypted local database

## Benchmark Devices

| Device | Why It Matters | Notes for Comparison |
|--------|----------------|----------------------|
| **Amazon Echo 4th Gen** | Original design target / "gold standard" for form factor, size, and everyday smart-speaker performance | Direct spiritual successor goal. Compare bass extension, midrange clarity, voice assistant usefulness (local vs cloud), presence detection, and overall "plug-and-play polish." |
| **Apple HomePod (2nd generation)** | Current high-water mark for computational audio and seamless ecosystem integration in a compact premium smart speaker | Compare spatial audio processing, automatic room correction, build quality/feel, and privacy posture. |
| **Sonos Era 300** | Leading multi-room / high-fidelity smart speaker with strong app ecosystem and Trueplay tuning | Compare stereo imaging, app experience, multi-room capability, and value. |

## Evaluation Criteria (to be used later)

- Bass extension and control relative to size
- Midrange naturalness and vocal clarity
- Treble smoothness and air
- Imaging / soundstage
- Overall dynamic capability
- Build quality, weight, and tactile premium feel
- Privacy and local-control advantages
- Ease of daily use within Home Assistant + Music Assistant
- Visual and industrial design coherence ("Quiet Quality")

## Success Criteria

We succeed if Electronic Bible matches or exceeds Echo 4th Gen on smart-feature usability AND matches or exceeds KEF LSX II on audio fidelity at 1/5th the price.

## Quiet Quality Design Philosophy

Inspired by refined minimalism and functional clarity, this project prioritizes honest engineering, acoustic performance, and timeless aesthetics while remaining fully local and privacy-focused.
